WELCOME TO SWEET BITE

Baking Happiness, One Treat at a Time

Sweet Bite Bakery is a bakery business based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. We offer a delightful selection of birthday cakes, wedding cakes, cupcakes, pastries and freshly baked treats.

We believe that every celebration deserves something special. That is why we focus on delicious flavours, attractive designs and friendly customer service.

Whether you are celebrating a birthday, planning a wedding or simply craving something sweet, we are here to make your moments more memorable.
